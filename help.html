OG Grow Journal - User Guide

Welcome! This guide will help you track your plants easily.

📱 Getting Started
  1. Tap + to create a new grow
  2. Enter strain name and plant count
  3. Pick type (Autoflower/Photoperiod)
  4. Set germination date
  5. Choose veg/flower durations
  6. Tap Save 🎉

⚡ Auto-Arrange

Tap ⚡ Arrange to automatically position all pots:

  • Optimizes space using smart bin-packing algorithm
  • Places largest pots first, then fits smaller ones
  • Maintains gaps between pots for airflow
  • Tap again to try different arrangements
📅 The Timeline

Your grow timeline shows all phases and entries:

🌱 Day 1 Day 90 🎉

Phase Colors

  • 🟣 Purple = Germination
  • 🟢 Light Green = Seedling
  • 🟢 Green = Vegetative
  • 🟣 Pink = Flowering
  • 🟠 Orange = Flush
  • 🟡 Yellow = Harvest

View Modes

  • List view = Chronological week-by-week
  • Grid view = Current week prominent, others in 2-column grid
  • Toggle with the 📋/🔲 buttons in the toolbar

Week Navigation

  • Tap week headers to expand/collapse
  • Current week auto-expands with "TODAY" badge
  • Scroll marker in progress bar shows your position
🖼️ Photo Gallery & Slideshow

View your grow journey as a fullscreen slideshow. Tap 🖼️ Gallery in the header.

Navigation

  • Swipe left/right or use arrow buttons
  • Dots at bottom = jump to any slide
  • Keyboard: ← → arrows, Escape to close

Auto-Play Slideshow

  • Tap ▶ to start automatic playback (3-sec per slide)
  • Tap ⏸ or Space to pause
  • Great for reviewing progress or showing off your grow!

Phase Timeline

  • Colored bar shows all grow phases
  • Moving marker indicates current slide position
  • See exactly where each photo falls in your grow journey

Slide Info

Each slide shows: Day number, Week, Phase, Entry title & notes

Tip: Add photos weekly to build an amazing visual timelapse of your grow!
✏️ Adding Entries

Quick Way

Tap + and choose: 💧 Water, 🌿 Feed, ✂️ Top, 🪴 Transplant, 🍃 Defol, 🌀 LST, 🌸 Pistils, 🚿 Flush

Detailed Entry

Tap any day on timeline → fill title, notes, photo → Save

⏰ Setting Reminders

Keep track of important tasks:

  1. Open a grow → Tap ⚙️ Settings
  2. Tap "Reminders"
  3. Add task, date, and buffer days (1-7 early notification)
  4. Save and get notified in advance

When a reminder appears, mark it as done to complete the task or dismiss it if not needed. Perfect for staying on top of watering, feeding, and milestones.

💡 Quick Tips
  • DLI Calculator: In the entry form, click the 💡 Calc button next to DLI to open the calculator. Enter PPFD and hours, then "Use This Value" to set it automatically.
  • Photos: Add weekly for an amazing timelapse in the gallery
  • Quick add: Use the + button shortcuts instead of typing
  • Multiple plants: Select which plant each note is about
  • Grid view: Great for seeing the current week at a glance
  • Reports: Generate a shareable HTML report of your entire grow
  • Reminders: Set buffer days for tasks like watering and feeding to stay on schedule

☁️ Sync across devices (optional)

The diary works completely without an account — that stays true. Sync is an extra you switch on yourself if you want the same diary on your phone, tablet and computer, and a copy that survives a lost phone or a cleared browser.

🔐 What happens to your data

  • Encrypted on your device before anything is uploaded (AES-256), with a key derived from your password.
  • We cannot read your diary. Not the notes, not the photos. What lies on the server is a block of ciphertext — also for anyone who ever got hold of that database.
  • Servers in the EU (Frankfurt). Only your email address is needed, no real name.
  • You are asked first: nothing is uploaded until you tick the consent box.

🔑 Your recovery key

When you turn sync on, the app shows a recovery key once — it looks like RCVR-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX. Write it down.

  • It is the only way back into the cloud copy if you forget your password.
  • Not even we can reopen it for you — that is the point of the encryption.
  • Lost both? Your diary on your devices is untouched. The app offers to throw the unreadable copy away and start a fresh one.
  • You can hand yourself a new key any time: Settings ⚙️ → ☁️ Sync → 🔑 New recovery key.

📱 Setting it up

  • Settings ⚙️ → ☁️ Sync across devices → create a free account
  • Tick the consent box → the first upload starts, photos are compressed on the way
  • On the second device: sign in, enter your password once to unlock the copy — your grows arrive
💡 Changing your password later? The cloud copy stays readable and nothing is re-uploaded.

🗑️ Turning it off again

  • Delete synced data: removes the copy and the photos from the server. Everything on your devices stays exactly as it is.
  • Delete my account permanently: account, cloud copy and photos are gone for good. Your diary stays on your devices.
  • Switch sync off and the copy is deleted by itself after 60 days.
🌱 Sync is a safety net, not a requirement. Without an account nothing ever leaves your device — and the JSON backup (Settings ⚙️ → Download JSON) keeps working either way.
❓ FAQ

Where is data saved? → In your browser, persists between sessions

Works on mobile? → Yes, phones, tablets, computers

Delete a grow? → Open grow → 🗑️ trash icon

Edit an entry? → Tap it in the timeline

Red pot border? → Pot too big, resize or move it

Do I need an account? → No. Everything works offline without one; sync is optional

Can you read my diary when I sync? → No, it is encrypted on your device first

Forgot my password? → Reset it, then unlock the cloud copy with your recovery key

Lost the recovery key too? → Your diary on this device is fine; start a fresh cloud copy

🆘 Troubleshooting

App not working? → Refresh page (F5 or pull down)

Lost data? → Restore from Settings → Import Backup

"Unlock your cloud copy"? → This device has no key yet — enter your password, or your recovery key

Sync says "offline"? → Nothing is lost, it catches up by itself once you are back online
